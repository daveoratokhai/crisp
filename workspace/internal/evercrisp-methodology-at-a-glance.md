## Intake

Intake is the first stage of Strategy/Discovery, where an intake chatbot interviews each respondent individually to understand the client's business and where value is leaking. It runs in two passes: a wide net across every respondent, then a deep dive with the people who live a given workflow day to day. It hands assessment raw, per-respondent material (workflow mentions, pain points, named systems and tools), each tied to who said it and how directly they experience it.

## Assessment

Assessment applies Evercrisp's outside-in analysis to the intake material, using research on the company, its market and competitors, read against the Value Flow Engine framework. Related mentions are clustered into one canonical picture per workflow, with provenance kept, and presented back to executives in a 90-120 minute discussion. It hands the workshop a clear focus and gives leadership a reason to bring the broader team in.

## Workshop

The workshop widens the lens to a broader cross-section of the team, the people who run the day-to-day workflows, in a facilitated session of a day to a day and a half. The client's own people name and prioritize the problems and workflows they want addressed, building on the clustering from assessment. It hands the opportunity backlog a raw, organization-wide list of everything worth addressing.

## Opportunity Backlog

The opportunity backlog groups everything from the workshop by theme and prioritizes it, separating large opportunities that need executive sponsorship and an Evercrisp build from smaller problems the client's team can learn to solve through training. It stays traceable to what the workshop surfaced, even when the client renames or reframes it. It hands implementation one opportunity at a time, in priority order, and gets revisited as new workflows surface or priorities shift.
