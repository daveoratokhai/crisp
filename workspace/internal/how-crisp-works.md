Crisp is where Evercrisp's documents, SOPs, skills and client work live, for people and agents alike.

## Local and published

Everything starts **local**: a file in your workspace folder, visible only to you. Publishing sends a document to the team. Nothing publishes on its own.

| State | What it means | What you see |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Local | Only you can see it | Dotted edge, *Local* chip |
| Published | The team sees this version | Solid teal edge, publish time |
| Unpublished changes | You have edited it since publishing | Orange *Unpublished changes* chip |

## Two ways to find things

Every client document also belongs to a process stage, so it appears under both **Clients** and **Processes** in the sidebar. Same document, two routes to it.
